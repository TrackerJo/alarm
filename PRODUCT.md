# Product

## Register
product

## Users
People who want a simple browser utility to find and connect to nearby Bluetooth Low Energy devices.

## Product Purpose
Let the user choose a nearby BLE device using the browser picker, see selected devices, connect, and disconnect.

## Brand Personality
Simple, clear, calm. The user requested a simple dark interface.

## Design Principles
Keep discovery and connection actions obvious. Describe real browser limitations. Show connection state and errors clearly. Keep device data local to the browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use native buttons, visible focus indicators, readable contrast, live status announcements, responsive layout, and reduced-motion support.
